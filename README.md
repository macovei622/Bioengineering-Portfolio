# Computational Bioengineering Portfolio 🧬⚙️

**Author:** Illya Macovei  
**Domain:** Biomedical Engineering, Computational Modeling, Neuroengineering  
**Tech Stack:** MATLAB, Simulink, Signal Processing, PDEs  

Welcome to my portfolio of computational bioengineering projects. My focus lies at the intersection of biomechanics, tissue engineering, and neuroprosthetics, utilizing MATLAB and Simulink to model complex physiological systems and optimize biomedical designs.

## 🚀 Projects Overview

### 1. [Neuroengineering & Prosthetics: EMG Gesture Classification](./Project_1_EMG_Control)
A signal processing pipeline for electromyography (EMG) data to classify hand gestures for virtual prosthesis control.
* **Focus:** Bio-signal filtering, feature extraction, intention decoding for neuroprosthetics.
* **Tech:** MATLAB, Signal Processing.

### 2. [Biomechanical Modeling of the Knee Joint (Simulink)](./Project_2_Knee_Biomechanics)
A dynamic computational model analyzing the passive stabilization of the human knee joint during a normal gait cycle.
* **Focus:** Gait analysis, non-linear ligament viscoelasticity, ACL tear simulation.
* **Tech:** MATLAB, Simulink (Analytical Kinematics/Dynamics without visual solvers).

### 3. [Tissue Engineering Scaffold: PDE Oxygen Diffusion & Topology Optimization](./Project_3_Scaffold_Diffusion)
A custom vectorized Finite Difference Method (FDM) solver for the reaction-diffusion equation to analyze hypoxia in 3D-bioprinted scaffolds.
* **Focus:** Mass transfer, PDEs, Topological Optimization, Tissue Necrosis elimination.
* **Tech:** MATLAB (Vectorized Numerical Computing, Jacobi Iteration).
